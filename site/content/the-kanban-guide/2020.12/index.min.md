## What Dis Booky For

Dis booky wanna be da one togethery reference for da tribe, by setting a teeny-minimum set of rules for Kanban. It build on da Kanban basics to hold up da whole big rainbow of banana-giving and big-boss headache.

In dis doccy, "Kanban" mean da whole bundle of ideas writted here.

## What Is Kanban, Den?

Kanban is a planny for making da banana zoom-zoom betta through a process, using a looky, pully system. Banana can mean lotsa things (like what da customer need, what da user need, what da company need, what da big blue planet need).

**Kanban got tree big do-dos:**

- Makey and looky da worky-flow
- Manage da worky bits in da worky-flow, all da time
- Fix-fix da worky-flow

Dese do-dos togethery make a **Kanban system**, and da peeps who join in is **Kanban system peeps**.

## Why Use Kanban?

Kanban look hard at **zoom-zoom** — da maybe-banana moving through a system. It wanna make **giv-da-right-stuff**, **no-wastey** and **no-surprisey** as good as dey can be:

- **Giv da right stuff**: Giv customers what dey want, when dey want it.
- **No wastey**: Use da monies and minion-power da best way.
- **No surprisey**: Can guessy da banana-giving close enough, even with da wobbly unknowns.

Kanban push da fix-fix along by making peeps ask betta questions earlier-earlier. It fit almost any worky-flow and almost any job-kind.

## Kanban Brainy-Think

Kanban build on old-and-goody brainy-think:

- Wibbly-wobbly and quality checky-check

Kanban can holdy-hand with other banana-giving ways and methody-methods.

## Da Kanban Do-Dos

### See Da Work: Makey and Looky Da Worky-Flow

Da shared understandy of zoom-zoom is called da **Definitiony of Worky-Flow (DoW)**. It must-must got:

- Say-so for what da worky bits is
- Say-so for started and finished
- Da states from started to finished (WIP)
- How da WIP get bossed
- Out-loud zoom-zoom policies
- Time Expecty (SLE)

Da **Kanban boardy** is da looky-version of da DoW. Dere is no bossy rules for how it look — see-through is da whole point.

### Manage Da Work: Bossing Da Worky Bits

Dis got do-dos like:

- Bossing da WIP
- No letting bits pile-pile up
- Stopping bits from getting oldy-oldy for no reason
- Unstucky da stucked work

Regular luks (but no must be formal) is cheered on.

#### Bossing Da Work In Progresso

WIP must-must be bossed out-loud, often with **WIP Limits** (numbers or tokens). Dis make a **pully system** — bits get pulled only when dere is room.

Any exceptions must-must be writted out-loud in da DoW.

#### Time Expecty (SLE)

SLE = chunk o' time + how-sure number (like, "85% of worky bits finish in 8 days").  
Use oldy **cycly time** to work it out, and looky-show it on da boardy. Use a guessy if yu got no data yet.

### Fix Da Work: Making Da Worky-Flow Betta

Teams keep fixing da worky-flow to get a betta balance of giv-da-right-stuff, no-wastey and no-surprisey.

Fixes can be teeny-steppy or BIG, and should happen whenever needed — no only on a set beaty.

## Kanban Measure-Bits

Da four teeny-minimum zoom-zoom measures:

- **WIP**: Work started but no finished
- **Throughputty**: Worky bits finished per chunk o' time
- **Worky Bit Oldy-ness**: Time since a bit started
- **Cycly Time**: Time from started to finished

Looky-show dese measures with whatever charty-charts help. Teams may use extra local numbers too.

## Last Blabla

Da Kanban do-dos and measure-bits is **no-can-change**. Half-doing it is no Kanban. Other techniques can be gluey'd on, but da minimum do-dos, da numbers, and da spirit of making banana big must-must stay.

## Oldy Story of Kanban

Kanban come from da **Toyota Production System** and big-brain peeps like **Taiichi Ohno** and **W. Edwards Deming**. Da now-now brainy-worky Kanban got borned in 2006 at **Corbis** and growed up through world-wide work-togethery.
