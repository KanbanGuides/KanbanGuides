About Kanban Guides

Kanban Guides is the home of The Kanban Guide and the Open Guide to Kanban, with online editions, translations, and supplied PDFs.

The Kanban Guide provides a minimal reference. The Open Guide to Kanban is an expanded adaptation for knowledge work. Their authorship and edition histories remain distinct.
